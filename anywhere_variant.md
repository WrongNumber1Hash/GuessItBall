# **GuesItBall — Playing Card Edition**

*No custom cards required*

## Materials

* 1 standard 52-card deck (no jokers)
* 1 coin per player
* Pencil and paper (or notes app) for tokens/scorekeeping
* Timer app on phone or watch
* 6-sided die (optional)

---

## Player Roles

* 2 - 6 Players
* One Player is also the Bookie, who tracks tokens and bets, and the Bank. They are a scribe but they get 1 token for performing the task.
* Players start with 100

Players can form teams and rotate the position of coach, choosing the plays each turn. 

## Card-to-Plays Mapping

You can use any cards, as long as each player or team of players agree on the values for the cards relating to plays. You can also call out choices instead of using cards. The human player (in case of playing AI) always calls out first, after both sides have locked their play choices. 

### Number & Face Cards

| Card   | Play           | Description                                           |
| ------ | -------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| 2      | Play #1        | Play 1 – Power / Sweep                                |
| 3      | Play #2        | Play 2 – Interior Run                                 |
| 4      | Play #3        | Play 3 – Speed / Positioning                          |
| 5      | Play #4        | Play 4 – Breakaway Play                               |

---

## Optional Depth Rules (Recommended)

These rules add flavor but can be ignored for simpler play.

### Card Color (Red / Black)

| Color | Meaning  | Example Use                                         |
| ----- | -------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Red   | **Pass** | The Offense chooses a pass play. To stop the play, the Defense must pick the number and the color. |
| Black | **Run**  | The Offense chooses a running play. To stop the play, the Defense must pick the number and the color. |

If the defense matches the color and the number, then there is a chance for an interception or a fumble. Pick a card from the pile, and if it is an ace, then it is a turnover.

---

### Suit Meaning

| Suit            | Benefit                 | Explanation                          |
| --------------- | ----------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Hearts          | **Add +1 to results**   | Offensive Line gets you an extra yard|
| Diamonds        | **Add +2 to results**   | Player shows some nifty footwork to get extra yards|
| Spades          | **Add +3 to results**   | When did it happen?                        |
| Clubs (Clovers) | **Add +4 to results**   | How did you express or handle the feeling? |

> These are optional rules for adding some complexity.


# **GuesItBall — Pen and Paper**

First player to 45 pts wins

## Materials

- Pencil or Pen

- Paper

## Player Roles

* 2 - 6 Players
* One Player is also the Bookie, who tracks tokens and bets, and the Bank. They are a scribe but they get 1 token for performing the task.

If you play in teams, then each person can take a different role

* Head coach - Decides Play Packages
* Offensive Coach - Calls offensive plays
* Defensive Coach - Calls defensive plays

Players can form teams and rotate the position of coach, choosing the plays each turn. 

## Points-to-Plays Mapping

You can assign any point values, as long as each player or team of players agree on the values relating to plays.

### Number & Face Cards

| Points | Play           | Description                     | Cost in Tokens |
| ------ | -------------- | ------------------------------- | -------------- |
| 3      | Play #1        | Play 1 – Power / Sweep          | 2              |
| 2      | Play #2        | Play 2 – Interior Run           | 1              |
| 4      | Play #3        | Play 3 – Speed / Positioning    | 3              |
| 5      | Play #4        | Play 4 – Breakaway Play         | 4              |

## Optional Depth Rules (Recommended)

These rules add flavor but can be ignored for simpler play.

### Play Color (Red / Black)

| Color | Meaning  | Example Use                                         |
| ----- | -------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Red   | **Pass** | The Offense chooses a pass play. To stop the play, the Defense must pick the number and the color. |
| Black | **Run**  | The Offense chooses a running play. To stop the play, the Defense must pick the number and the color. |

## Downs

3 downs per possession

5 downs per Quarter

## Special Rules

Play #4 can only be played after Quarter 1

Each Player starts at 0 points

## Punt

If a player punts, they receive 4 points and possession passes to the opposing team

## Kickoff

If a player kicks off, they receive 3 points. If a player receives, they receive 1 point.
