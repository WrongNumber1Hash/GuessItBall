## Guess It Ball

🏈 Turn-Based Grid Football Game

A **turn-based, strategy football game** played on a grid.
Players secretly choose plays, manage space, avoid blocked squares, and out-think their opponent across four quarters.

This can be played by 2-6 players, with more players taking turns as coach, being able to deploy different plays (offense or defense) or customize their play results. You can play vs a human opponent or play team vs team, or play against a GenAI chatbot.

## What's the Big Idea

Learning about strategy and how the opposing player thinks is the name of the game. With only 3 or 4 plays to choose, players try to either guess the play their opponent will choose if they are defending, or choose the mix of plays that gets them downfield if they are on offense.  No prior knowledge of American football is needed, just modify the rules and game to make it as simple or complex as you want. The goal of the game is to score first but the time can be adapted to be short or long, to cover 1 drive or the whole 4 quarters of a game.

## What Kind of Game is It?

It is based on a sport, American football, but can be adapted for any sport involving a ball and field, where the aim is to score a goal. GuessIt ball aims to have two players create strategies, and guess which plays will allow them to score. The psychology is a part of the game, in that thinking of the opponent's strategy and choices helps a player decide their choices.

1. Players can play against a human player or an AI player. 
2. Players flip a coin to choose to kick off or to receive.
3. Players choose a story card before the start of each quarter to see what game conditions will be. 
4. Players on Offense take 4 Offense Play Cards and 1 set of 2 color cards.
5. Players on Defense take 4 Defense Play Cards and 1 set of 2 color cards.
6. Players use a pencil (or pen) and scratch paper to diagram plays

## Sample of Play

Human Player
--
Human Players declare simultaneously by putting down cards, can flipping them over at the same time.

1. Human players chooses a play card secretly along with a color card. They note their play choice on the scratch paper.
2. Human players diagram 1 of 5 play choices [|_ | _| -| |-]
3. When both human players are ready, they put down 3 Hint Cards, only one of which is true.
4. Human players show number of the play, the color card and the diagram of the play they wrote
5. Result of the play is decided and marked on the board
6. Down is progressed and time of play continues
7. Offensive Player decides to keep current Play Card or take a new one and discard an old one. This is secret.
8. Defensive Player decides to keep current Play Card or take a new one and discard an old one. This is secret. 


### Sample Play 1 — Standard Offensive Play (Defense Chooses Wrong)

**Game state**

* Quarter: 2
* Down: 1st & 10
* Ball position: (36, K)
* Drive direction: toward Row 5

**Offense declares**

* Play: **4 — Breakaway**
* Color: **Black**
* Intended path: 2 steps right, then forward

**Defense declares**

* Play: **2**
* Color: **Red**

**Resolution**

* Defense guessed the wrong play number → *Defense chooses wrong*
* Base movement for Play 4 applies: **+5 forward**
* Play 4 allows up to 2 lateral steps → lateral move of 2 right is legal
* Lateral movement resolves first, then forward movement

**Result**

* Ball moves 2 columns right, then 5 rows forward
* All traversed squares become **blocked**
* Down advances by 1

## Why Play This Game?

It can be a fun way to plan a strategy to win the game, and some of it will depend on reading your opponent's choices! With only 3 or 4 choices each round, how fast can you as a coach decide the plays that will move the ball. With a larger group of people, it becomes a team game where 1 person can be the Offense coach, and 1 person can be the Defense coach, and another person can be the Head coach.

## Game Components Explanation

### 10 Story Cards

| Card Name | Flavor Text | Effect |
|-----------|-------------|--------|
| **Home Crowd Momentum** | *The stadium is roaring.* | First successful offensive play each quarter gains +1 point. |
| **Rain Game** | *Sloppy footing changes everything.* | Play #4 can only score 4 instead of 5. Punt gives 5 points instead of 4. |
| **Trash Talk** | *Somebody crossed the line.* | Betting minimum doubles this quarter. |
| **Two-Minute Drill** | *Clock pressure changes playcalling.* | Final quarter only: Failed defensive guess gives offense +1 bonus point. |
| **Defensive Lockdown** | *The defense came to play.* | Defensive successful stop (correct number) awards +1 bonus point. |
| **Playbook Leaked** | *Scouts got their hands on something.* | The first time each team uses Play #2 this quarter, it scores 1 extra point. |
| **Windy Day** | *The wind makes everything harder.* | Offensive plays #3 and #4 can only score 3 instead of their normal value. |
| **Rivalry Week** | *This one means more.* | Bets that are accepted this quarter pay out +1 point. |
| **Short Week** | *Fatigue sets in.* | Each team must discard 1 Timeout for this quarter (if available). |
| **Big Play Expectations** | *The crowd wants excitement.* | If Play #4 is not used by the offense this quarter, they lose 1 point. |
