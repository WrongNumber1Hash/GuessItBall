# Creating a Draft and a Team

Each Player has 100 points to create player groups, and 100 dollars to draft players for their teams.

## 🏗️ Section 1: Game Setup & Roster Creation

### 1.1 The 100-Point Roster Pool

Each player has **100 points** to distribute across 10 distinct position groups across Offense, Defense, and Special Teams. Points are split into two baselines:

* **Max Skill:** Determines the performance threshold of that position group on the field.
* **Max Stamina:** Determines the physical endurance and depth of that position group.

### 1.2 Official Team Roster Template

Players must allocate all 100 points during the draft, after creating the players for the draft pool. Values chosen at the draft represent the initial value and can be added to, if extra points are earned.
