# Quick Start — Chat Setup

This page explains how to **start and run a game cleanly in chat**. It assumes the full rules exist elsewhere and focuses only on what players must agree on before play begins.

If you follow this page step by step, the game will run smoothly without rewinds or disputes.

---

## Step 1: Decide Who Kicks Off (Coin Toss)

1. Each player chooses **Heads** or **Tails**
2. A virtual coin is flipped
3. The winner chooses one:

   * **Kick**
   * **Receive**
4. The other player gets the remaining option

**Second Half Rule**

* At the start of the 2nd half, the player who did **not** win the opening coin toss chooses whether to kick or receive

---

## Step 2: Confirm the Win Condition

Choose **one** before kickoff:

* **Default:** First player to score a **touchdown** wins
* **Variant:** After 4 quarters, the player with the **greatest total forward advancement** wins

> If using the variant, both players must explicitly agree before the game starts.

---

## Step 3: Quarter & Down Structure

* The game has **4 quarters**
* Each quarter contains **15 downs**
* A **timeout** causes the *next down* not to count against the quarter limit
* A **quarter warning** is announced **4 downs before the end** of the 2nd and 4th quarters

Quarters regulate pacing, not real time.

---

## Step 4: Field Orientation & Drive Direction

* Each player always drives toward the **opponent’s goal line**
* Teams **switch ends after the 1st and 3rd quarters**
* On a **turnover**:

  * The ball stays where it is
  * **Drive direction reverses immediately**

> “Forward” and “backward” are defined by drive direction, not absolute board rows.

---

## Step 5: Play Secrecy Protocol (Required)

To preserve fairness in chat, all plays use **simultaneous reveal**.

**Protocol**

1. Both players privately choose their play
2. Both type: **Play locked**
3. In the next message, both reveal:

   * Play number
   * Color (if applicable)
   * Intended lateral movement

No changes are allowed after a play is locked.

---

## Step 6: Kickoff Resolution

* Kickoffs occur:

  * At game start
  * At the start of the 2nd half
  * After touchdowns

Kickoff resolution follows the **kickoff rules** in `rules.md`, including:

* Position and color selection
* Match-based return distances
* Optional lateral adjustment
* Touchback handling

---

## Step 7: Begin Play

Once the kickoff is resolved:

* Possession is established
* The receiving player begins at **1st & 10**
* The ball is live

The offense may now declare their first play.
